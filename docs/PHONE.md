# The phone

A smartphone the player takes out anywhere: **P** (changeable in the controls), the
"Open your phone" buttons at home and in town, or a notification banner. **P** again,
**Esc** or "Put the phone away" puts it back. The home bar goes to the home screen; the
back arrow goes back. While it is out in a 3D place, the character holds it up and stands
still, and the keys go to the phone; walking resumes when it is put away.

## What is on it

The home screen: the game's clock and date, a status bar (signal, battery), the
wallpaper, an app grid with badges, and a dock (Messages, Contacts, Maps, Camera).

| App      | Connected to                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Messages | Friends' messages and invitations (accept, decline), texting and calling them: the existing social rules                                                                                                                               |
| Contacts | Friends, and everyone the player has got to know at work and in town, with how they see the player; a call: ringing, then they answer (colleagues only in working hours) and talk, with the same trust and memory as talking in person |
| Calendar | This week's meetings, plans accepted with friends, a takeaway on its way, the current story objective                                                                                                                                  |
| Maps     | The neighbourhood's places, where the player is, the story's destination; from the street, Go there                                                                                                                                    |
| Bank     | The balance, this week's money in and out, every transaction: the game's own money and ledger                                                                                                                                          |
| Career   | The player's level and XP, and job applications (made on the laptop at home)                                                                                                                                                           |
| Food     | Three restaurants and their menus; ordering is the home's takeaway (paid once, a courier to the front door, tracked)                                                                                                                   |
| Circle   | A social feed from friends and colleagues, different each day; likes are kept                                                                                                                                                          |
| Browser  | Websites written for the game: the company, a job board, the supermarket, local services, learning                                                                                                                                     |
| Camera   | A photo of the 3D view, kept on this device (a dozen, newest first), in a gallery                                                                                                                                                      |
| Settings | Wallpaper (six), ringtone (three, previewed), notifications on or off                                                                                                                                                                  |

**Notifications**: a banner (with a sound) for news that arrives while playing: a
friend's message, the courier at the door, a meeting in ten minutes, the next story
objective. Each once; off in Settings.

**Saved**: the settings and likes in the career's save (`life.phone`, with defaults for
older saves); photos on this device only. Nothing else is stored twice: the phone reads
the game's money, messages, meetings, story and people.

**Sounds**: opening, closing, taps, notifications, a message, a ringtone (three), a call
connecting and ending: all generated, through the existing sound settings.

## Limits

- Calls are short exchanges, not voice; texts can be sent to friends only.
- No transfers between accounts (there is one account).
- Maps does not draw a route in 3D; Go there walks the player to the place, as the
  Places list does.
