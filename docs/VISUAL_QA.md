# Visual and audio QA

How the game's look and sound are checked, and what was found in the
graphics and sound upgrade (November 2026). The pictures are taken by
browser tests in the real game, not mock-ups.

## How to take the pictures

- **Every career and place at High:** `SHOTS_DIR=<folder> npx playwright test
tests/e2e/visual.shots.spec.ts` (add `PLACES_LIMIT=1` for a quick pass). It
  sets the High graphics setting, so the pictures show what a player with a
  graphics card sees (soft shadows, reflections, materials), even on a
  computer that draws 3D in software.
- **The home and the city:** `tests/e2e/cityhome.shots.spec.ts`.
- **Each workplace activity at its object:** `npm run shots:environments`.

## Known limits

- The characters are built from simple shapes and posed in code: they are
  tidy and clearly dressed for their work, but not detailed, realistic
  models. Real skinned models would mean replacing every animation.
- Spoken dialogue uses whatever voices the browser has: quality varies, and
  a browser with no English voices speaks with its default voice.
- Only a few sounds are placed in the world (the doorbell); most background
  sounds are spread across the stereo field rather than tied to objects.
- The tests check that the right sound scene plays and that sound comes out;
  how it sounds was judged by design, not by listening in the tests.
