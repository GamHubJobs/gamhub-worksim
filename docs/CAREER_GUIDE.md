# Your career: a guide for players

This guide covers the parts of WorkSim that follow your development over time:

- your performance record;
- achievements;
- your desk;
- readiness assessments.

For cases, see [CASES_GUIDE.md](CASES_GUIDE.md); for the portfolio, see
[PORTFOLIO_GUIDE.md](PORTFOLIO_GUIDE.md).

## Your performance record

Open **Career** on the dock. The **Performance record** sums up your work in the
simulated workplace:

- clients and customers;
- budgets and approvals;
- workplace incidents;
- meetings and deliverables;
- work with other departments;
- leadership;
- relationships;
- your progression.

Every figure is worked out from your saved work, and says how. There are no made-up
scores: a figure is a count, a total or an average of things you actually did.

**Level by level** shows your tasks and cases at each level you have held, so you can
see how your work has developed. When you are promoted, earlier levels keep their
record.

## Achievements

Achievements recognise real work. There are 19, in four groups:

| Group                    | For example                                            |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Professional performance | Pass 5 tasks first time; pass an incident case         |
| Workplace conduct        | Earn three colleagues’ trust; hand over work perfectly |
| Leadership               | Share out a team’s work well; review it carefully      |
| Career progression       | Reach the level where you lead a team; use your tools  |

Each achievement says exactly how to earn it and shows your progress, for example
“2 of 3”. Once earned, it is yours for good, with the day you earned it. There are no
achievements for logging in, no streaks and nothing to buy. A short line in your day’s
log tells you when you earn one.

## Your desk

Achievements and promotions earn things for your desk:

- a plant;
- a mug;
- a lamp;
- a photo frame;
- a pinboard;
- the colour of your chair.

Choose them under **Your desk** in Career, or use **Arrange your desk** at your desk
in the office. They appear at your desk in the 3D office. They are only for looks:
they do not change your work.

## Readiness assessments

Each career has two **readiness assessments**: one at level 2 and one at level 3.

### What an assessment asks for

An assessment is a practical piece of work for the responsibilities of your level.
It takes 3 hours of office time and asks you to:

- investigate a situation;
- work out a figure;
- make a decision;
- **write a short report in Docs**;
- send a clear message.

For example, an accountant reconciles the bank and explains the errors. An IT analyst
diagnoses an outage, fixes it and writes the incident report.

### Results and retakes

- **You need 80% to pass.** The report counts, so it cannot be skipped.
- **Feedback.** You see your strengths, what to work on and why, with the expected
  answer for each part.
- **Retakes.** If you don’t pass, practise and take it again. A new attempt uses
  another version, with different figures and details. Every attempt is kept.
- **Promotion.** From level 2, your next promotion needs your level’s assessment.
  Career shows it as a requirement, with a link. Levels you already hold are never
  taken away.
- **Portfolio.** A passed assessment goes into your portfolio with its report.

Open them from **Readiness** on the dock.

These assessments show what you have demonstrated in WorkSim’s simulated workplace.
They are not qualifications or certificates.

## In your portfolio

The **Progress report** in Portfolio, and its PDF, include:

- your simulated workplace experience (only figures above zero, each with its source);
- your readiness results;
- your achievements;
- your next steps, including the assessment your next promotion needs.
