# Installing WorkSim and using it offline

## Installing

WorkSim can be installed like an app, so it opens in its own window and from your Start
menu, dock or home screen. Nothing is downloaded from an app store and nothing is paid
for.

- **Chrome or Edge (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS):** open WorkSim, then click the
  install icon at the right of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose
  **Install GamHub WorkSim** (Chrome) or **Apps → Install this site as an app** (Edge).
- **Safari (macOS 14 or later):** choose **File → Add to Dock**.
- **Firefox:** desktop Firefox does not install web apps. Use WorkSim in a tab: it
  still works offline once it has been opened online.

To remove it, uninstall it like any app, or from the browser’s apps page. Uninstalling
the app does **not** delete your saved progress, but clearing the site’s data in the
browser does. Make a backup first (see below).

## What works offline

After WorkSim has been opened once with an internet connection, it keeps a copy of its
own files on your device. Then, without a connection:

- WorkSim starts, and your careers, tasks, cases, files and portfolio open;
- all your work is saved on this device as usual;
- every app and tool works, including the 3D office, Slides, Code, SQL and the
  profession tools;
- Grid Craft, Word Craft and Deck Craft, the office spreadsheet, word processor and
  presentation app, work once they have been opened online. Each is downloaded the first time it is opened, not
  with the rest of WorkSim, as they are large. Until then, the basic editors open
  the same files;
- you can download a backup file and export portfolio PDFs and ZIP files.

When you are offline, a small notice at the top says so. Nothing else changes.

## What needs a connection

- **The very first visit,** to download WorkSim.
- **A career you have never opened on this device.** Each career’s content is
  downloaded the first time you open it. It is kept for offline use after that. If you
  try to start a new career while offline, WorkSim tells you to reconnect.
- **New versions,** which arrive the next time you are online.

## Your data stays on this device

WorkSim is local-first:

- your progress is stored in your browser on this device, and is never sent anywhere;
- going back online does **not** upload or sync anything;
- there is no cloud copy, so the only copy of your work is on this device, plus any
  backups you download.

Download a backup regularly from **Storage & backup**. WorkSim reminds you if you
haven’t for a week.

## Updates

When a new version of WorkSim is ready, a notice says **A new version of WorkSim is
ready** with an **Update now** button.

- Nothing changes until you choose to update: you can finish what you are doing first.
- **Update now** saves all your work, then reloads into the new version. Your progress
  is kept.
- If your latest changes cannot be saved yet, the update waits and tells you so.

## If something goes wrong

- **WorkSim behaves strangely after an update:** open **Storage & backup** and choose
  **Repair app files**. This downloads WorkSim’s files again (you need a connection).
  Your saved work is not touched.
- **“WorkSim could not load the content for this career”:** you are probably offline
  and opening a career for the first time on this device. Reconnect and choose **Try
  again**.
