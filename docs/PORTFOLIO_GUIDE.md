# Your portfolio: a guide for players

Everything you pass in GamHub WorkSim is kept as evidence of what you can do. This guide
explains where to find it, what it shows and how to share it.

## Where it is

Open **Portfolio** on the dock (after Career). It has five sections:

| Section             | What you find there                                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Work**            | Every task and project you have passed, newest first    |
| **Skills**          | Your level in each skill, why, and what to improve      |
| **Progress report** | A summary of a career save, with recommended next steps |
| **Profile**         | A one-page profile you can present, in your own words   |
| **Export**          | PDFs and a ZIP with everything, ready to save or share  |

Use **Career save** at the top to look at one career, or at all of them.

## How work gets into it

- **Only passed work appears.** Pass a task and it is added straight away, with the files
  exactly as you handed them in, the score, and every check with why it matters.
- **Your best attempt is kept.** If you submit again and do better, the portfolio keeps the
  better version. A weaker attempt only adds to the list of attempts.
- **The files are frozen copies.** Changing your working files later does not change them.
- **Projects** (at the top level of each career) appear once every stage has passed. A
  project links to each of its stages.

If something you passed before is missing, open **Storage & backup** and click
**Rebuild portfolio**. It adds anything missing and never changes what is there.

## What you can change

On a piece of work’s page you can:

- include it in your exports or leave it out,
- **feature** it (it is shown first, and on your profile),
- add a **note** about what you did and learnt.

You cannot change scores, dates, checks or files: they are the evidence.

## Skills

Each skill has a level, calculated only from passed work where the skill is a main part
of the task:

- **Demonstrated**: one task,
- **Practised**: two tasks,
- **Proficient**: three tasks, one at your career’s second level or above, with an average
  score of 80% or more.

Each skill says why it has its level and links to the work behind it. **To improve** lists
the checks you did not fully pass in your latest attempts.

## Your profile

Write your **display name**, a **headline** and a short **summary**. Everything else
(your simulated career progress, skills, featured work, tools and experience) comes from
your passed work. You can show or hide those sections, but not edit them. The preview
shows exactly what will be exported.

## Exporting

In **Export**:

- **Everything (ZIP)**: a folder with a README, the portfolio PDF, the competency report,
  and each piece of work with its own PDF and its original files (spreadsheets as CSV,
  reports and slides as web pages and text, code as source files).
- **Portfolio (PDF)** and **Competency report (PDF)**.

Each piece of work also has **Download as PDF** and **Download original files**, and the
profile can be downloaded as a PDF or a web page. Everything is made on your device;
nothing is uploaded.

## Being honest about it

Every export says that the work was done in **GamHub WorkSim, a workplace simulation**,
with simulated tasks and data. It shows real skills you practised, but it is **not** a
record of employment, a qualification, a certification or a reference. Please describe it
that way when you share it: for example, “practised in a workplace simulation”.

## Keeping it safe

Your portfolio is stored in this browser on this device, like the rest of your work.
**Download a backup** in Storage & backup now and then: backups include the portfolio, and
restoring one brings it back exactly.
