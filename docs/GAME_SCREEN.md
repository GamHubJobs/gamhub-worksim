# The game screen

At work, at home and in town, the 3D world is the screen: it fills the browser window
below a slim top bar (the name, the clock, the career, Story, settings, help, storage),
with no page around it and no scrolling. Everything else sits over the world:

- **The drawer** (top left): what can be done here (the rooms and their activities, the
  places in town, plans, money, the evening's log) in a panel over the world. **Hide ◂**
  folds it away to a small button, and it stays folded (kept on this device).
  Conversations, story scenes and dates opened from it stay on screen either way.
- **The HUD** (bottom right): six small bars (hunger, hygiene, social, fun, energy,
  stress) and shortcuts: **Phone** (P), **Map** (the phone's map) and **Menu** (Esc).
- **Prompts** (bottom centre): "Press E to …", only when there is something to do.
- **The pause menu** (Esc, when no box is open and the mouse is not captured): Resume,
  Map, Missions (Story), Relationships, Character, Inventory, Settings, Help, Save &
  backup, Main menu (continue, Story Mode, every career). Each opens over the world and
  goes back to exactly where the player was.

The 3D view is never rebuilt by opening or closing a menu, the phone or the drawer.
Without 3D, the drawer is the page, as before.

## Limits

- Laid out and tested for desktop windows (1024 × 700 and up). Phones and tablets are
  not claimed.
- The office keeps its own panel and Places bar (it was already full screen).
